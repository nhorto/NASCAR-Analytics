04 / The naming shortlist

A name you can say on race day.

Creative candidates. Domain, trademark, and App Store availability have not been checked.

Where I would start.

Looplab + the telemetry loop is my first pick: it connects the product’s loop data to a clear, compact racing symbol. The tire badge is the most expressive route for your original idea. Stint is my favorite fresh-name direction for a short, confident app identity. Pick the name and symbol independently; the workbench lets you mix them.

Exploration, not a final brand decision. These editable concepts are for choosing a direction; the store layout and downloads are design studies, not an App Store submission package. The existing app colors come from the project’s design system.
